Dr. Andrés R. Saravia Moya

Consultant in Intensive Care Medicine | ECMO & Neurocritical Care Specialist | Healthcare Innovation Advisor

Dr. Andrés R. Saravia Moya is a consultant intensivist based in Madrid, Spain, with over 11 years of experience in adult critical care. He is internationally recognized for his leadership in extracorporeal life support (ECMO), neurocritical care, and tele-ICU system development.

Dr. Saravia has overseen high-volume ECMO programs, launched remote ICU networks across multiple hospitals, and trained key opinion leaders in advanced life-support strategies. He is a published co-author in JAMA (TRAIN trial), a top-20 peer reviewer for JACEP Open, and an active advisor to MedTech startups focused on critical care innovation.

Fluent in Spanish, English, and French, he lectures in ECMO and critical care at postgraduate level and collaborates actively with the European ECMO and digital health communities.
